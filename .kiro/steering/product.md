# Product — Finlux

## What is Finlux
Finlux is an agentic personal finance application. Its tagline is:
"Your AI agent that brings clarity to your personal finances."

Finlux is not a passive dashboard. It is an **AI agent** that proactively helps
users understand, organize, and improve their personal finances through natural
conversation and automated reasoning over their financial data.

## Core value proposition
- **Clarity**: turn raw transactions and accounts into clear, understandable insight.
- **Agency**: the AI agent takes initiative — categorizing, summarizing, alerting,
  and recommending — rather than waiting for the user to dig through reports.
- **Trust**: handle sensitive financial data responsibly, transparently, and securely.

## Target users
Individuals who want to understand and improve their personal finances without
needing accounting expertise. They expect a conversational, assistant-like
experience rather than spreadsheets.

## Primary use cases (initial scope)
- Connect / import accounts and transactions.
- Automatic categorization of transactions.
- Conversational Q&A over the user's finances
  (e.g. "How much did I spend on food last month?").
- Spending summaries, trends, and budget tracking.
- Proactive insights and alerts (unusual spending, upcoming bills, goals).

## Non-goals (for now)
- Executing real-money transfers or payments on behalf of the user.
- Professional financial, tax, or legal advice. Finlux informs; it does not
  provide regulated advice.
- Multi-tenant enterprise / B2B features.

## Experience principles
- Conversation-first, with supporting visualizations.
- Explain reasoning: when the agent makes a claim about the user's money, it
  should be traceable to the underlying data.
- Privacy and security by default — financial data is highly sensitive.
